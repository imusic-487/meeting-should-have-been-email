One line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email funny.

Run `python3 main.py` and it writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> We spent an hour in a meeting deciding what to put in the email that should have replaced the meeting, then emailed a summary nobody read.

The joke is the loop: the meeting exists only to decide what goes in the email that should have replaced it, and the summary email that follows is the one nobody reads.
